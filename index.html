          MANIFESTO

          AFTER LIGHT

          Real stories. Brief light. No measure of grief.

          AFTER LIGHT is an interactive 3D space for encountering real, source-linked dispatches about conflict, catastrophe, sudden loss, and lives remembered. Each dispatch appears as a headline and a sourced sentence, then enters the sky as a brief firework. The visual is an interpretation; the event and its facts remain with the source.

          A space designed for attention. The journey begins among 57 real star patterns and descends into an open void: no floor, horizon, or mirror. Visitors can orbit, draw closer, or travel toward a burst. Depth, motion, and sound invite exploration without flattening the news into a feed.

          A visual language of transience. A firework rises, opens, and fades. Its light creates a pause—not an explanation, decoration, or unit for grief. When the burst ends, the story remains one click away.

          Every shell is a configuration, not a special effect. Sixty-two documented archetypes shape each burst’s form, colour, motion, and sound. Each is defined by calibre, emitter chemistry, star count, burn time, drag, and break asymmetry; reference image, video, or audio can be added without changing the rendering code. One dispatch, one shell. Up to 100,000 particle slots.

          The boundaries are part of the design. No casualty counters, maps, coordinates, rankings of suffering, invented stories, or sensationalized summaries. No Day / Week / Month tabs: tragedy is not a period filter. The only number in the sky is frame rate, a measure of the machine—not the world. Every burst leads back to its original Wikipedia article.

          Grief and hope can coexist. Care, courage, and persistence do not erase loss; loss does not erase the people who keep going. This experience makes room for both without claiming to resolve either.

          Reduce motion. Adjust the volume. Pause the sky. Explore, then return to the account behind the light.
